# Sheaf — Play Store Listing Copy (ASO-optimised)

Google Play ranks on the **title**, **short description** and **full description** (no hidden
keyword field), plus install velocity, ratings and Android vitals. Keywords below are placed
where Play weights them most, and phrased naturally, because keyword stuffing breaks Play's
metadata policy.

## App name (30 char max)
**Sheaf: PDF Reader & Editor** (26)

- The brand stays "Sheaf". "PDF Reader & Editor" is a generic descriptor, which Play allows and
  which carries the highest-volume search terms ("pdf reader", "pdf editor").
- Don't add "Adobe", "Acrobat", "best" or "#1": these break the metadata policy.

## Short description (80 char max)
**Read, sign, fill & scan PDFs. Night mode, OCR, no ads. Private and on-device.** (78)

## Full description (4000 char max)
Sheaf is a fast, private PDF reader and editor for Android. Read, annotate, sign, fill forms
and scan documents, all on your device. No account, no ads, and your files never leave
your phone.

READ COMFORTABLY
• Smooth PDF viewer with pinch and double-tap zoom
• Reading mode reflows text so it fits your screen. No more side-scrolling on a phone
• Night, Sepia and Light themes that recolour the page itself, for easy reading in the dark
• Full-text search and table of contents navigation
• Tabs: keep several PDFs open and switch between them instantly
• Picks up exactly where you left off in every document
• Two-pane layout on tablets and foldables

ANNOTATE & SIGN
• Highlight passages and draw with a pen in six colours
• Add sticky notes anywhere on a page
• Create your signature once and sign any document in a tap
• All annotations in one list. Jump straight to any of them
• Share a flattened copy with your markup and signature built in

FILL PDF FORMS
• Fill in interactive PDF form fields right on the page
• Save and share the completed form

ORGANISE PAGES
• Rotate, delete and reorder pages
• Save the result as a new PDF

SCAN TO PDF
• Scan paper documents with automatic edge detection and cropping
• Multi-page scans go straight into your library as clean PDFs

OPEN PROTECTED PDFS
• Open password-protected PDFs. The unlocked copy is deleted as soon as you close it

SHEAF PRO: ONE-TIME PURCHASE, NO SUBSCRIPTION
• OCR: turn scans into searchable, selectable text
• Compress PDF: reduce file size for email and upload
• Password-protect a PDF with AES encryption

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sheaf works offline and processes your files locally. Scanning and OCR use Google ML Kit
on-device. We never see your documents. No ads, no analytics, no account.

ACCESSIBLE
Works with TalkBack. Every page, tool and control is labelled, and reading mode text scales
up to 200%.

Sheaf is for anyone who just wants their PDFs to work: students, professionals, contractors,
landlords and teams who sign, fill and read documents every day.

## Localisation (biggest organic lever after the title)
Translate the listing into DE, FR, ES, PT-BR, IT, JA and KO (largest PDF-app markets). The app UI
is English-only today, so localise the listing first, then the strings.

## Ratings & vitals (what keeps an app in the top 5%)
- Ask for a review with the Play In-App Review API after a success moment (e.g. the 3rd signed
  or filled document), never at launch.
- Keep the user-perceived crash rate under 1.09% and the ANR rate under 0.47% (Play's
  bad-behaviour thresholds). Watch Android vitals weekly after launch.
- Reply to every 1–3★ review within 48h. Replies measurably lift ratings.
