Sheaf — Privacy Policy

Sheaf is published by Plentrix Ltd (Ireland), the data controller for this app.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Sheaf ("the app") is a PDF reader and toolkit for Android. Sheaf is built to keep your documents on your device. It has no user accounts, shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics or tracking.

What the app accesses

  • Your files: only files you open or import; they remain on your device. Edited, filled, compressed, protected, or scanned copies are stored privately or shared only when you choose.
  • Camera (scanning): uses the Google Play services ML Kit Document Scanner, which runs in Google's own UI and processes images on-device. Sheaf does not require the camera permission itself.
  • OCR: performed on-device with Google ML Kit; recognized text becomes a searchable layer in your PDF and is not sent to us.
  • Signatures: signatures you draw or photograph are saved as images in the app's private storage on your device, excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, and leave your device only inside documents you choose to share or save.
  • Photos and pictures: only pictures you select in Android's photo picker (or share to Sheaf) are read, to turn them into PDFs or signatures. Sheaf has no access to the rest of your photos.
  • Saving and exporting: copies and JPG, Word, Excel and text exports are written only where you choose, or for page pictures to the Pictures/Sheaf album on your device.
  • Translate: tapping Translate hands the text to the Google Translate app on your device; nothing is sent unless you tap it.
  • Purchases (Sheaf Pro): handled by Google Play Billing. Sheaf stores only a local "Pro unlocked" flag and never receives your payment details.
  • Network: used only for Google Play (purchases) and Google Play services components. Sheaf does not send your documents over the network.

Data we collect

Sheaf does not collect, store, or share personal data on our own servers, and we never receive your documents.

The Google ML Kit libraries used for scanning and OCR send limited diagnostic data to Google: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, app version), a per-installation identifier that does not identify you or your device, and performance/error metrics. Google uses this to diagnose and improve ML Kit, encrypts it in transit (HTTPS), and does not share it with third parties. It never includes your documents, images, or recognized text. ML Kit data disclosure.

Diagnostic log

To help fix problems, Sheaf keeps a small log of errors and crashes on your device (technical details only: app and Android version, device model, and error messages; never your documents, their names or passwords). It is never sent automatically. It leaves your device only if you tap Settings → Share diagnostic log and choose where to send it. You can clear it at any time in Settings.

Third-party services

Google Play services / ML Kit (scanning, on-device OCR) and Google Play Billing (purchases), each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Sheaf is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
