# Sheaf — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Sheaf is published by **Plentrix Ltd** (Ireland), the data controller for this app.

Sheaf ("the app") is a PDF reader and toolkit for Android. This policy explains what the app does and
does not do with your information. **Sheaf is built to keep your documents on your device.**

## Summary
- Sheaf has **no user accounts**, shows **no ads**, and contains **no third-party analytics or tracking**.
- Your PDFs and their contents are processed **locally on your device**. Sheaf does not upload your
  documents to any server operated by us.

## What the app accesses
- **Your files:** Only files you explicitly open or import. They remain on your device. Edited, filled,
  compressed, protected, or scanned copies are written to the app's private storage or shared only when
  you choose to share them.
- **Camera (document scanning):** Scanning uses the Google Play services ML Kit Document Scanner, which
  runs in Google's own interface. Captured images are processed **on-device** to produce a PDF. Sheaf does
  not require the camera permission itself and does not transmit captured images to us.
- **Text recognition (OCR):** Performed **on-device** with Google's ML Kit. Recognized text is written
  into your PDF as a searchable layer and is not sent to us.
- **Signatures:** Signatures you draw or photograph are saved as images in the app's private storage
  on your device, excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. They are placed into a document only
  where you put them, and leave your device only inside documents you choose to share or save.
- **Photos and pictures:** Only pictures you select in Android's photo picker (or share to Sheaf) are
  read, to turn them into PDFs or signatures. Sheaf has no access to the rest of your photos.
- **Saving and exporting:** Copies, JPG, Word, Excel and text exports are written only where you choose
  (Android's "Save" screen) or, for page pictures, to the Pictures/Sheaf album on your device.
- **Translate:** Tapping Translate hands the selected text to the Google Translate app on your device.
  Nothing is sent unless you tap it; Google Translate's own privacy policy then applies.
- **Purchases (Sheaf Pro):** In-app purchases are handled by **Google Play Billing**. Google processes the
  transaction; Sheaf only stores a local flag indicating whether Pro is unlocked. We do not receive or
  store your payment details.
- **Network:** The app uses the internet only to communicate with Google Play for purchases and to allow
  Google Play services components (scanner/OCR models) to function. Sheaf does not send your documents
  over the network.

## Data we collect
Sheaf does not collect, store, or share personal data on our own servers, and we never receive your
documents.

The Google ML Kit libraries used for scanning and OCR send limited **diagnostic data to Google**:
device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, app version),
a per-installation identifier that does not identify you or your device, and performance/error
metrics. Google uses this to diagnose and improve ML Kit, encrypts it in transit (HTTPS), and does not
share it with third parties. It never includes your documents, images, or recognized text. Details:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

## Diagnostic log
To help fix problems, Sheaf keeps a small log of errors and crashes **on your device** (technical
details only: app and Android version, device model, and error messages; never your documents, their
names or passwords). It is never sent automatically. It leaves your device only if you tap
**Settings → Share diagnostic log** and choose where to send it. You can clear it at any time in Settings.

## Third-party services
- **Google Play services / ML Kit** (document scanning, on-device OCR) — see Google's Privacy Policy.
- **Google Play Billing** (purchases) — see Google's Privacy Policy.

## Children
Sheaf is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
